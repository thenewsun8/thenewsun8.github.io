Press Kit · OLDMAN – Off the Record (2026)

Logline

An anonymous hacker leaks the footage the world was never meant to see: after a pandemic "cure" that promised eternity, the old were discarded as empty shells — until one of them came back.

Synopsis

OLDMAN – Off the Record is a found-footage short assembled from leaked surveillance feeds, broadcasts and recordings, narrated by the hacker who stole them. The global pandemic is officially over. A corporation called Biolion did not sell a cure — it sold forever. But inside the bodies of those who took both the vaccine and the drug, something began to turn. The old were the first to change, and the first to be thrown away: collected as "empty shells" and fed to the incinerators that power the city. One of them did not burn. Nobody knew his real name. The cameras of the whole city began looking for a single man, and by the time they found him, his blood was already falling as rain.
